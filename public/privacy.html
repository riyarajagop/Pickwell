YOUR DATA

Privacy policy

Effective August 4, 2026

Pickwell does not require an account. Your taste profile, saved recipes, ratings, recent recipes, and meal log are stored locally on your device or in your browser.

Information stored by Pickwell

Pickwell stores the food preferences and restrictions you choose, dietary-filter settings, saved recipes, recipe feedback, recently displayed recipes, and meal-log entries. This information is used to personalize recommendations and show your history.

Network requests

Pickwell may contact Pickwell services and third-party food-data services to load recipe or ingredient information. Those requests may expose ordinary technical information such as your IP address and browser or device details to the service receiving the request.

Recipe and ingredient information may come from TheMealDB, USDA FoodData Central, and Open Food Facts. Images and original recipe links may be served by their respective providers.

Accounts, advertising, and tracking

Pickwell does not currently offer accounts, sell personal information, show third-party advertising, or use your Pickwell activity to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

Your locally stored Pickwell information remains until you clear it. In the app, open More and select Clear all local data. You can also remove it by clearing the site or app storage, or by uninstalling the app. Pickwell cannot recover information removed this way.

Children’s privacy

Pickwell is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated as Pickwell changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made. For help or privacy questions, use the developer contact listed on Pickwell’s App Store page and see the Support page.
