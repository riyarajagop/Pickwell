How do I change my recommendations?

Open Taste and mark foods you like, dislike, or cannot eat. You can also turn on dietary-pattern filters. Pickwell updates your recipe choices from that profile.

How do I clear my information?

Open More, select Clear all local data, and confirm. This resets your taste profile and removes saved recipes, ratings, recent recipes, and meal-log entries stored by Pickwell on that device or browser.

Why does a recipe or nutrition estimate look wrong?

Recipe and nutrition information comes from external food-data sources and may include estimates. Pickwell is a planning aid, not medical advice. Always check original labels and sources for allergens, dietary needs, certifications, serving sizes, cooking temperatures, and food safety.

Why is a feature unavailable?

Some ingredient searches and nutrition features need a network connection. Try again when you are online. If the problem continues, restart the app or browser and check whether other Pickwell features load.

Report a problem

Use the developer contact shown on Pickwell’s App Store page. Please include your device or browser, operating-system version, Pickwell version, what you expected, what happened, and the steps that reproduce the problem. Do not include sensitive health or personal information.
